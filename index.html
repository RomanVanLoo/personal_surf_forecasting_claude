Wind hits 32-48kph by noon and stays brutal through evening. Ericeira drops to 1.2-1.8m but with gale-force wind it’s unsurfable. All spots along the coast are a write-off from midday. The big W swell starts marching in overnight.

Best Advice for Monday

Dawn is the only window and it’s marginal. If you’re sore from Sunday, skip it guilt-free — there’s nothing to gain that you didn’t get yesterday. The gale-force wind from noon signals the start of a multi-day blowout. Rest up, work, and wait for Friday.

Tuesday 7 April — MASSIVE Swell Arrives

A huge 17-18s W groundswell slams the coast. Ericeira goes from 1.8-2.4m at dawn to 3-4.5m by noon and a staggering 4.5-5.5m (4.4m 16s, 9927kJ) by evening. Wind is 31-43kph all day. Supertubos: 3-4.5m noon building to 4.5-5.5m evening. Caparica: 2.4-3.7m noon, 3-4.5m evening. Everything is XXL and blown to pieces. Pure spectator day — if you want to see massive waves, head to Nazaré or watch the Ericeira cam from the couch. Probability only 50-60% at dawn, but 100% by evening — the swell is coming, it’s just a question of exactly when.

Wednesday 8 April — Still Huge & Windy

Ericeira holds at 3-4.5m all day on a 15s period with 7038kJ at dawn and 6256kJ at noon. Wind is relentless: 34-55kph. Supertubos: 2.4-3.7m dawn, 2.4-3m noon. Everything is still way too big and windy. Another day off the water. The swell starts to show signs of easing by evening but remains huge (2.4-3.7m with 6275kJ). Temperature drops to 8-13°C — cold and miserable out there even if you could surf.

Thursday 9 April — Easing but Blown Out

Ericeira drops to 2.4-3m at dawn (3.7m 13s, 4683kJ) easing to 1.5-2.1m by evening. The size is coming into range but the wind is still savage: 47-61kph all day. That’s gale force — completely unsurfable everywhere. Caparica 1.2-1.8m dawn, Carcavelos 0.9-1.2m dawn — both blown apart. Supertubos 1.2-1.8m with 33-49kph wind. No chance today. The wind needs another day to calm down.

Saturday 11 April — Blown Out & Uncertain

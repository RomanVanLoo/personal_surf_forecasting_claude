Tuesday 7 April — Storm Building, Stay Dry Today

Massive W/WNW swell building all day. Ericeira goes from 1.2-2m this morning to 4.5-5.5m by evening — that's 10,500kJ of raw energy with 33kph+ onshore wind. Everything is blown out. Carcavelos building to 2.4-3.7m by 6pm. Even the sheltered spots can't handle this. Not a single surfable window anywhere on the coast today.

Best advice for today

Watch the swell build from the cliffs if you're near Ericeira — it'll be spectacular by late afternoon. Save your energy for Thursday dawn, that's your next real window. The swell peaks overnight and starts easing Wednesday.

Wednesday 8 April — Massive, Mostly Unsurfable

Peak swell day. Ericeira 4.5-5.5m at dawn (8,423kJ, 100% consistency!) with 31kph wind — spectator-grade waves, far too big and windy to surf. All Ericeira spots maxed out. Even São Sebastião (2.0m max) is way beyond its limits. Carcavelos eases to 1.8-2.4m by evening with lighter 9kph wind, but that's still heavy barrel territory and gusty to 18kph.

Saturday 11 April — Gale Force, Unsurfable

A fierce storm system moves in. Winds build from 21kph at dawn to 59-80kph by afternoon/evening at Ericeira. Supertubos gets hit with 38-65kph. Even Carcavelos (further south and somewhat sheltered) sees 25-62kph. New swell arrives (1.5-2.1m building to 2.4-3.7m at Ericeira by evening) but with 67-80kph gusts it's completely shredded. Zero surfable windows anywhere on the Portuguese coast today. The noon/evening swell has very low probability (20-40%) too.

Best advice for Saturday

Stay well clear of the ocean. These are serious gale-force winds with massive gusts. Grab a coffee, watch some surf clips, rest your body. The storm continues through Sunday and Monday.

Sunday 12 April — Storm Continues, Unsurfable

The storm persists. Ericeira has a solid swell running — 2.4-3.7m at dawn (5,644kJ, 100% consistency) — genuinely impressive power. But wind is 59-69kph at dawn, easing only slightly to 43-54kph by evening. Every spot from Caparica to Peniche is blown to pieces. Good swell, terrible wind. What a waste.

Best advice for Sunday

Another enforced rest day. The swell is there but the wind makes it impossible. If you're near the coast, the raw ocean will be impressive to watch from a safe distance. Monday might offer a very marginal window as winds start to ease, but don't get your hopes up.

Monday 13 April — Still Windy, Marginal

Swell easing but still substantial — 1.8-2.4m all day at Ericeira with 2,982-3,676kJ. Good size, good energy, good period (13-14s). But wind is STILL 27-37kph all day. No region escapes it — Carcavelos 13-34kph, Supertubos 16-33kph, Caparica 13-34kph. The wind is coming down from the weekend gale but not fast enough. Maybe — MAYBE — if you're at Supertubos at dawn (16kph wind, 1.2-1.8m, 2,125kJ) there's something rideable, but it's 90 min away and still messy.

Best advice for Monday

Probably still a skip. The wind is easing each day now — Tuesday looks much better. Unless you're absolutely gagging for waves and willing to drive 90 min to Peniche for marginal conditions, rest up for the Tuesday noon window.

Tue 7 – Wed 8 Apr — MASSIVE W Swell

Carcavelos 1.8-2.4m rising to 2.4-3m (15 min from home, handles size well). Caparica 2.4-3m. This is the big one — Carcavelos dawn on Tue 7 could be the session. Wed still large but easing.
